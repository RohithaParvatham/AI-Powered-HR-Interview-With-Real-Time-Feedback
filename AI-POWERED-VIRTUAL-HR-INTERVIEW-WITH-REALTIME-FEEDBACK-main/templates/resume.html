Choose your interview format, then upload a resume. Job descriptions are matched against supported skills to prioritize relevant questions and identify skills to practice.

Accepted formats: PDF and DOCX, up to 5 MB. Use a text-based PDF; scanned image-only resumes cannot be read yet.

Your session has a countdown. Unanswered questions are marked skipped when time runs out.

Resume and job-description text are analyzed locally in memory and are not saved. Questions, answers, feedback, and detected skill names are stored in the app's local database. Opting into recording requires camera and microphone permission.
